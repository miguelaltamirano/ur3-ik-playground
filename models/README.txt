UR3 CB3, not UR3e. Six numerical revolute joints; wrist_3_joint is physically continuous, with a classroom search interval of [-2pi,2pi].
Robotiq 2F-85 is rigidly mounted through its official 11 mm adapter and fixed fully open.
classroom_tcp is the midpoint of area-weighted centers of the two inward fully open fingertip contact faces, extracted from official visual meshes and oriented with the gripper base. Upstream has no pad/TCP frames. It is an uncalibrated classroom convention.
This URDF omits visual/collision geometry and is for numerical verification. Browser geometry, source attribution and licenses are in dist/assets/robot/.
